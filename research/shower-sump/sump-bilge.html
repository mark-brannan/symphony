How to read it

  • Height zero is tab height: the plane the box rests on, where the two hull widths were taken. The sole is above it, the bilge bottom below.
  • The hull is drawn as two flat walls leaning 23 and 25 degrees below tab height, pulling together toward the bow at the taper the two widths give. Above tab height the walls flare out to meet the sole. The flare and the bottom are guesses guided by the photos.
  • The taper uses 3 1/2 in between the two widths. That is a working number, not a tape reading. With it, the hull at the box's forward end comes out narrower than the box, which cannot be true of a box that is sitting there. Read that overlap as the size of the error in the model.
  • Depths come from photo perspective, not from a tape held against the surface, so they carry the widest error: 2 to 3 in.
  • Hose and wire routes away from the box are guesses. Where they leave the box is from the photos.
