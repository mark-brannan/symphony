# Shower sump: the bilge around it

[`sump-bilge.html`](sump-bilge.html) is a 3D sketch of the bilge at the shower
sump, for seeing the space a fabricated platform would sit in. Open it in a
browser; no build. Inches.

The two hull widths at tab height (8 3/4 aft, 7 1/2 forward, taken as 3 1/2
apart) and the hull's lean from vertical (about 23 starboard, 25 port) were
taken aboard on 2026-11-21. Everything else is read off photos or guessed and
is drawn that way. Good for seeing the space, not for cutting parts.
