What the picture says about wiring room

    Where the sources disagree

    • The long pull, set C. A tape run under the ceiling reads 38 at the back of the aft jamb, with its start out of frame. Run straight back from the jamb it would pass the back wall by about 16. It may instead start on the back wall and turn the corner. The floor tape (set B) says 22 deep. The model uses 22 and draws the 38 as a dashed line.
    • Which wall is which. The index calls the wall with the height tape (set A) the back wall; the reader of the photos took it for the aft side wall. The transformer is drawn on the back wall, close to the aft side.
    • Inside width. No tape runs across the inside. The walls show just behind each jamb, so 20 is drawn against a 19 opening.
    • Doors. Hinges show on both jambs; no door is in any photo. Two leaves are drawn as a guess.
