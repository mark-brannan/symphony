    Where the sources disagree

    • Depth of the cabinet. Inside, near the floor: 10 1/2 to the back of the sill, 12 to its front (set I). Outside, at the aft end: 9 5/16 at outlet height (set K) and about 12 at the overhead (set N). Drawn as read: a plumb back wall at 12 inside, a leaning planked wall aft of the post.
    • Panel frame width. The 21 November hand-off note gives the frame as 0 to 15.5 and the fascia as 15.5 to 23. The close-up photo and the February tape both put the plate's edge at 17.4, the frame's rail end at 18.0, and the next joints at 19.7 and 21.6. The close-ups are used.
    • Which part swings. The model treats the finger-hole board as the door's own stile (door 1.0 to 19.7). February's notes left that open.
    • Zeus size. The photos give 8.75 wide; a maker's figure was not found in the project files.
