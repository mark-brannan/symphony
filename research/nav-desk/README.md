# Nav desk: space for the new wiring

The nav desk, the electrical panel and the cabinets behind and below them, as
3D sketches for planning the redesigned wiring: new terminals and two 24 in
DIN rails. The HALPI2 is one object in this space and may move.

- [`nav-desk.html`](nav-desk.html): the desk, the panel (it swings open on its
  left hinge) and the cabinet behind it.
- [`below-nav-desk.html`](below-nav-desk.html): the low cabinet under the desk,
  with the isolation transformer and the original terminal strips.

Open either in a browser; no build. Inches. Almost every number is read off
photos or guessed, and each page draws and lists which. Still to measure:
the depth behind the panels, the free room inside the HALPI2 case, and a tape
tying the lower cabinet to the desk.
