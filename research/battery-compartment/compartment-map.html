How far to trust it

  • The opening, walls, lips, heights and the fan hole are as measured. Expect them within about a quarter inch.
  • The hull is two straight slopes, fitted to where the floor stops being level, the two corner drops (within 3/8 of both) and the deep lip's overhang. No drops were taken along the walls, so it may be an inch or two off in between. The fit-four planner uses this same hull.
  • The corner drops are taken as measured from the top of the rim. If they were taken from the underside of the lip, the hull at those corners is 2 lower than drawn.
  • The outboard battery is drawn lying on the slope, as in the photo. Its exact turn is adjusted so it fits between the walls.
  • Four of the five wall holes have no measured position. They are drawn dotted where the notes and photos suggest.
