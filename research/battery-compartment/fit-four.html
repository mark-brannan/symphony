Inches. Only the hull is solid: a battery drops until it meets the floor or hull (or, with "rest on batteries", a battery below) and passes through everything else, so all four can always be placed. Whatever overlaps, or comes within 1/8, turns red and see-through, and the table says by how much. "Resting" lays one face flat on a surface, then spins it about that surface's normal; "along the hull line" runs the long edge parallel to the skewed rise line. "Free" turns it any way at all; the planner draws the shelf that would hold it there. A battery on the hull needs a stop on its low edge; one on a shelf, the shelf. Keys: arrows move (Shift: 1 in), PgUp/PgDn lift, [ and ] spin, 1–4 pick, Ctrl+Z undo. The layout is kept in the address, so a copied link reopens it. The shell numbers in fit-four.js are best guesses from photos, not confirmed aboard; see README.md.
